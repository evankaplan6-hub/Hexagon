# A Polymarket maker leg — measured, not built (2026-09-12)

Phase 4 of `ops/rework-plan-2026-09-12.md`. The question: does resting quotes on Polymarket's CLOB
have the same problem that took the Kalshi maker's backtest from +$2,187 to +$210 — the queue
already sitting at the touch — and what does a resting order there actually earn? Measured with
`tools/pm-maker-scan.js` (read-only: Gamma listing, CLOB book, data-API prints) on the pool the
Kalshi maker would quote: non-sport, a week or more from resolution, mid inside 8–92c, drawn from
the top 1,000 Polymarket markets by 24h volume. 133 markets. The Kalshi column is the same
measurement on the 273-market pool `tools/maker-rank.js` scores and on the 24 the live desk
picks by trade rate, from `data/fly/rank-listing.json` and 14 days of `rank-trades.jsonl`.

## The queue

|                              | Kalshi pool (273) | Kalshi live 24 | Polymarket pool (133) | Polymarket top 24 by rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| queue at the touch, median   | 349    | 15,372 | 1,428  | 2,771  |
| trades / day, median         | 6      | 113    | 36     | 162    |
| contracts / day, median      | 438    | 14,938 | 9,764  | 33,067 |
| queue clears in, median      | 1.0d   | 1.0d   | <1h    | <1h    |
| clears inside a day          | 137 (50%) | 12 (50%) | 109 (82%) | 24 (100%) |

Like for like — the 24 busiest on each venue — Polymarket's queue is a fifth of Kalshi's and its
flow is twice Kalshi's, so a fresh order at the touch is reached in hours rather than a day. The
README's "queue clears in < 1 day" bucket, where all of the surviving Kalshi edge sat, is the
whole Polymarket book. On this axis Polymarket is the better venue to make markets on, and it is
not close.

## What a resting order earns

- **The spread.** 81 of 133 books are one tick wide, as on Kalshi. But 22 markets tick at 0.1c,
  where one tick is nothing to earn; the political markets with 1c ticks look like Kalshi's.
- **The rebate.** Makers pay no fee. On the 97 fee-enabled markets takers pay
  `rate × p × (1 − p)` per contract (rate 3–7%, 5% on the Fed) and 25% of that is rebated to the
  maker they crossed, daily. At the mid that is **0.19c a contract, median** — a fifth of a tick,
  only on fills where a taker crosses us, only on fee-enabled markets. Geopolitical markets, a
  third of the pool, charge nothing and rebate nothing.
- **Liquidity rewards.** 105 of 133 markets pay a daily pool (median $10/day, the Fed and the
  Clarity Act $200–500/day) to orders resting within `rewardsMaxSpread` of the mid at
  `rewardsMinSize` or more (median 50 shares). This is the real income on Polymarket, and the
  split among makers is **not disclosed** ("how helpful your orders are in terms of size and
  pricing compared to others"; a day pays only if it reaches $1). It cannot be modelled from
  outside, only earned and measured.

## What this does not measure

Phase 2's finding on Kalshi was that the level which clears fastest is the level that gets
swept, and a sweep through a resting quote — run-over — is the fill that lost the money. Nothing
above says whether Polymarket's fast-clearing touches are swept the same way. The data API
carries every print with its taker side, so the same replay (`tools/maker-replay.js`, quotes
reconstructed from prints) would answer it in a day. It was not run, because of the next section.

## Recommendation: no-go, for a reason the numbers cannot change

The measurement says go. Two things outside it say no:

1. **Access.** The CLOB measured here is `polymarket.com`'s, which is not available to US persons;
   `polymarket.us`, the CFTC-regulated product with its own API and catalog, lists none of these
   markets (README: season-long futures only, as of 2026-09-09) and has no maker rewards program
   on them. There is no venue to take this live on from here. A paper desk against public data
   would be measuring a strategy with nowhere to run.
2. **The income is a black box.** The spread is one tick and the rebate is a fifth of one. What
   would make a Polymarket maker pay is the rewards pool, whose formula is undisclosed. A backtest
   of it would be a guess at the guess.

Park it. Revisit if either `polymarket.us` lists the political catalog with a rewards program, or
US access to the CLOB changes. When that happens the first step is the run-over replay above, not
a desk, and `tools/pm-maker-scan.js` is the yardstick to re-run first — the queue numbers here are
one afternoon's snapshot.
